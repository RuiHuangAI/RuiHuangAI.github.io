# Academic Page Story of Rui Huang

## About Me

![Avatar](images/RuiHuang.png)

I am a third-year undergraduate student in the [Everest Project](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/) Computer Top-Talent Experimental Class at the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/) (UESTC), majoring in Computer Science (2023–2027). I rank 1/164 in my major and 1/334 across the school.

I am currently a Research Intern at **Tencent Hunyuan** through the Qingyun Program, working with [Tianyu Pang](https://p2333.github.io/) on World Models and Multimodal Reinforcement Learning. My research interests sit at the intersection of **LLM SFT/RL and reasoning, image and video generation, and unified multimodal models**. I have also worked on AI for smart grids and remote-sensing image fusion in earlier projects.

I am eager to discuss research collaborations and open to research opportunities (industry or academic, including onsite). My [CV](files/cv/Resume_Rui_Huang_04.pdf) is here.
